### One qualification, so "benign" is not read as "transient"
It is STRUCTURAL, not a race that resolves. The peer announces its tip exactly once per block and the
value it carries is the PARENT of the header it is delivering; it then says nothing until the next
block. That is why the condition is 762/762 rather than intermittent, and why `peer_age_ms` reaches a
full inter-block interval (40 s on the last line). `durable_servable_tip == followed_peer_tip` is
therefore unsatisfiable while following this peer at the tip — measured now, where LIVE-2c inferred it.
